3D Lantern Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. Applies to the 3D Lantern Studio app for Windows, published by L3G3ND82.

The short version

  • 3D Lantern Studio runs on your PC. It works offline.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no email address to enter.
  • Pictures you open and designs you make stay on your PC. They are never uploaded or sent to us or anyone else.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting in the app.

Information the app uses

3D Lantern Studio uses only what you give it while you design:

  • Pictures you choose. You can open your own pictures to turn them into lithophane panels. A picture could show a person, so it could count as personal information. The app reads the picture on your PC to make the panel and a preview.
  • Your designs and settings. Lantern sizes, roof and light choices, panel settings and print settings.
  • Files you export. 3MF and STL files, including an assembly guide PDF inside kit files.

The app doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other details about you.

Where it is stored

Your pictures, designs and settings are saved in the app's own data folder on your PC, so your work is still there the next time you open the app. Files you export are saved wherever you choose. By default that is Documents\3D Lantern Studio.

None of this leaves your PC unless you move or share the files yourself.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, upload or store your pictures, designs or settings on any server.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting tools in the app.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal information, because the app doesn't send us any.

Internet use

The app doesn't connect to any server while you use it. If you click a link to a website, it opens in your own web browser, and that website's privacy policy applies there.

The Microsoft Store

Microsoft handles downloading, installing, updating, any purchases, and ratings and reviews for apps in the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes that information under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

As the publisher, we may see reports from Microsoft such as download counts, ratings and reviews you choose to post, and crash summaries that Windows collects on its own. These reports don't include your pictures or designs.

Your controls

  • Remove a picture or panel at any time inside the app.
  • Use File, Start fresh to clear every saved picture and setting at once. It asks before clearing anything.
  • Exported files stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Security

Because your information stays on your PC, it is protected by your Windows account and your device's own security. Keep your PC up to date and use a sign-in for your Windows account.

Children

3D Lantern Studio isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that work with information in a new way, such as Store subscriptions or sending a bug report, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
